Mockup Studio

Drop artwork onto a garment, position it, export a proof-ready mockup.

Choose a garment, then drop your artwork in →

Blend multiplies the art by the garment's own shading so it sits in the folds instead of looking pasted on. Drag on the canvas to move; scroll to resize.

Then run
python3 scripts/new-proof.py --job 1042 --art <file> …
to turn it into a customer approval page.
